About the workshop

Fifteen years of small batches

Marlowe & Hide started as two cutting tables and a single stitching machine. It's grown into a workshop of nineteen makers — but the batches are still small on purpose.

What we make

We manufacture full-grain leather handbags across three lines — everyday totes, crossbody bags, and structured clutches — sold to independent boutiques and through a small made-to-order counter at the workshop itself. Every hide is inspected by hand before it's cut, and every finished bag carries the initials of the maker who closed its final seam.

This management portal exists because that hand-made process still needs coordinating: someone has to know which hides are left, which orders are due Friday, and which station a given bag is sitting at right now. That's what the dashboard, inventory, and production pages on this site are for.

Mission

To manufacture leather goods that are built to be repaired rather than replaced, made in batches small enough that every maker's craft is still visible in the finished piece.

Vision

A workshop where growth never outpaces craft — where the systems we use to track materials and orders stay as considered as the bags themselves.

Values

Material honesty, fair pay per piece, and a paper trail — now a digital one — that any maker on the floor can read at a glance.

How the workshop got here

  • 2011

    Founded as a two-person cutting table making custom totes for a single boutique in Nairobi.

  • 2015

    Added the crossbody line and hired the first dedicated stitching team.

  • 2019

    Moved into Workshop No. 4, with separate cutting, stitching, and finishing stations.

  • 2023

    Introduced the structured clutch line and a formal quality-check stage before packing.

  • 2026

    Replaced the paper production ledger with this internal management portal.
