The workshop's records, stitched into one system.

Marlowe & Hide handcrafts leather bags in small batches. This portal is where our cutting, stitching, and finishing teams track materials, orders, and production — replacing the old paper ledgers on the workshop floor.

Open the dashboard About the workshop

Three ways the portal keeps pace with the floor

Every stitch starts with a material and ends with an order. These are the core modules the team opens each morning.

Module 01

Inventory & Materials

Live counts of hides, hardware, thread, and lining, sorted by supplier and reorder threshold — so cutting never waits on stock that isn't there.

Module 02

Order Tracking

From a boutique's wholesale order to a single custom commission, every job is tracked from cutting table to courier, with status visible to the whole team.

Module 03

Production Scheduling

Cutting, stitching, hardware-fitting, and quality pass each get a station and a queue, so a bag's progress through the workshop is never a guess.

Built for a workshop, not a warehouse

Marlowe & Hide makes roughly forty bags a week across three lines: totes, crossbody, and structured clutches. That's small enough that every bag has a maker's initials on the inside seam — and this portal is sized to match: no modules the floor doesn't use, no reports nobody reads.

It's also a teaching build: a frontend-only mock-up of the management system, made to demonstrate page structure, navigation, and interface design before any of it is wired to a real database.

What's inside the portal

A public-facing site (this one) for context, and an internal dashboard for the three modules above — inventory, orders, and production — plus a page showing the server's current date and time, built in PHP for the coursework requirement.
